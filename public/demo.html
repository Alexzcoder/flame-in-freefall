PROBLEM

Moon bases are planned at 30 % oxygen, but no solid material in NASA's public data has been burned in microgravity above 22.2 %.

SOLUTION

1,624 NASA microgravity fire tests and 1,882 report pages in one checked dataset, a flame simulation tuned on NASA runs, and answers that cite their source.

DEMO

Set any cabin air, watch the flame change in the flame lab, light objects in a 3D habitat, and ask Ember, who cites NASA reports page by page.
