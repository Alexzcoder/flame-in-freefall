How the risk and confidence are calculated
  • Distance compares a habitat with a real test: 1 unit is about 5 points of oxygen or a doubling of airflow. Pressure is not used for solid fuels, because those tests have no measured pressure.
  • Risk is the share of the 5 nearest tests of the same material in which a flame ignited. It is indicative only. Most tests (BASS-II) started with a burning flame and then reduced the airflow until it went out, so they say little about where a flame would not start.
  • Confidence is red for any gravity above 0 (no Moon or Mars data exists), for materials with no tests, for pressures far from cabin air, and when the nearest test is more than 3 units away. Green needs a test within 0.5 units and at least 3 within 1.5. These thresholds are judgement calls, not NASA values.
  • Habitat airflow values are placeholders, not verified ventilation speeds.
  • Not a physics simulation: it only reads back what the tests showed.
